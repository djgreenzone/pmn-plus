Tongan Gamer and Team Clinch Victory at Esports World Cup

Hisoka, Biffle and Shifty take Team Falcons to the first-ever $1,000,000 Warzone Esports World Cup title in Riyadh.

Queenie Taua'aAugust 1, 20242 min read

Biffle, Hisoka, and Shifty have proven themselves to be an unstoppable force in Warzone. Congratulations to these incredible players for winning the final game and securing the first-ever $1,000,000 Warzone Esports World Cup! Shifty, Biffle, and Hisoka are your inaugural Warzone $1 MILLION World Cup Champions! Team Falcons claimed the Call of Duty Warzone EWC title with an impressive Grand Final points tally of 227.9.

Hisoka, of Tongan descent, has made a significant impact on the Polynesian community, inspiring many with this achievement. This accomplishment underscores the message that if one can do it, so can others - so can you, so can I, so can we all! They are the very first team to win a game championship at the Esports World Cup 2024. Team Falcons secured their first title at the Esports World Cup, the most prestigious global gaming event, held in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia in early July.

Dominating the popular game "Call of Duty: Warzone," they earned 1,000 points, topping the Esports World Cup rankings. With a flawless performance, winning all four of their games on LAN, they made history. This is truly impressive and it's fantastic to see our own excelling in various fields, especially in the gaming world. Congratulations to Hisoka and Team Falcons! T’z UP!
