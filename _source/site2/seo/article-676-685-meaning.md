# 676 and 685: What the Numbers on Tonga and Samoa Gear Mean

If you've been to a Pacific rugby league game, you've seen the numbers: 676 across a red shirt, 685 on a blue one, written on flags, cars and Instagram bios. They're not jersey numbers. They're the international calling codes for Tonga (+676) and Samoa (+685).

## From phone code to identity

For the Tongan and Samoan diaspora in New Zealand, Australia and the United States, the codes became a short way to say where home is. Three digits fit on a hat, a tattoo or a username, and anyone from the islands reads them instantly. Over time 676 and 685 turned into badges of pride, especially on game days when Mate Ma'a Tonga and Toa Samoa take the field.

## Why the numbers matter in rugby league

Pacific nations have changed international rugby league. Players with Tongan and Samoan heritage choosing to represent their families' islands turned Mate Ma'a Tonga and Toa Samoa into teams that draw some of the loudest crowds in the sport. Wearing 676 or 685 in the stands says you're part of that.
