Tribal Combat Unleashed: Roman Reigns vs. Solo Sikoa for the Ula Fala on WWE Raw’s Netflix Debut

The Ula Fala is on the line on January 6 as Monday Night Raw begins its Netflix era at the Intuit Dome.

Queenie Taua'aJanuary 1, 20252 min read

The countdown is on! WWE Raw is about to make its epic debut on Netflix for Monday Night Raw, and the buzz is real. We can’t wait for everything the show has in store, but there’s one match we’re especially excited about: Roman Reigns vs. Solo Sikoa, with the legendary Ula Fala on the line. This monumental battle will take center stage on the January 6 WWE Raw Netflix premiere, and it’s set to be a game-changer!

Roman Reigns is gearing up for a fierce Tribal Combat against Solo Sikoa, and he’s calling on the entire family to witness the fight. The stakes are higher than ever, and this isn’t just any match... it’s a showdown that will decide who the TRUE tribal chief is.

January 6, 2025, is shaping up to be a historic night for WWE fans as Raw officially moves to Netflix, marking the beginning of a new streaming era for the wrestling world. This milestone is part of WWE’s massive 10-year, $5 billion deal, and the first episode will stream live from the Intuit Dome in Los Angeles at 8 p.m. ET. Music icon Travis Scott, whose tracks fuel the Raw brand, has also been invited by Triple H to attend. While there’s no official word on the episode’s duration, The Wrestling Observer suggests it’ll still run for a thrilling three hours.

Who will walk away with the Ula Fala?!? Solo or Roman? Will the Original Tribal Chief reclaim it? We’ll just have to wait and see come January 6! WWE fans are in for one unforgettable ride. It’s going to be a showdown for the ages!
