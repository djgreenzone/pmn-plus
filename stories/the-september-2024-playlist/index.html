The September 2024 Playlist

Ten tracks to refresh your rotation this month, kicking off with a little “Brown Love”.

Queenie Taua'aSeptember 1, 20242 min read

Kicking off this playlist with a little ‘Brown Love’— because every great vibe starts with a little sweetness and soul.

  1. Brown Love — A Dough
  2. Uma — J Boog
  3. Be Roots — Hooliganz
  4. Lifetime — Topshakaz
  5. Independent Girl — Tomorrow People
  6. Mango Tree — Aaradhna
  7. Iesu — Shane Walker
  8. Roll Up — Kehlemi x Stone II
  9. Easy — A.R.T
  10. Our Love — Nōne Liufau x Siaosi x Drumwise
