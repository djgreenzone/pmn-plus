Kiwis Claim Victory Over Toa Samoa in Pacific Cup Final

New Zealand take the 2025 Pacific Cup 36-14, closing out a tournament that brought Pacific nations together in blue, red and black.

Queenie Taua'aNovember 1, 20252 min read

The New Zealand Kiwis have taken the 2025 Pacific Cup, defeating Toa Samoa 36-14 in a thrilling showcase of Pacific rugby league talent.

October through November is always an exciting time for international footy. The Pacific tests bring our nations together. Pride, parades, banter, and fun moments flood social feeds as families, friends, and fans from all over unite to cheer, celebrate, and connect. It is a period that reminds us just how much rugby league can bring our people together.

A special shoutout to everyone putting on the jersey, whether blue, red, black, or any color. Representing your people, families, culture, or homeland is what makes this sport so special. Those who step onto the field carry the weight and the heart of our communities, and in doing so, unite Pacific people across the globe.

Looking ahead, the Rugby League World Cup 2026 is just around the corner. It is anyone’s game, and excitement is building. Until next time, keep celebrating the moments, the pride, and the joy that international footy brings to all of us.

Congratulations to all teams who competed in the Pacific Championships this year. Your talent, heart, and dedication brought fans together and made this year’s tournament unforgettable.
