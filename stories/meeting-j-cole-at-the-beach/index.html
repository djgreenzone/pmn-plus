Meeting J. Cole at the Beach

Thirty minutes, one laptop, and a verdict on the Kendrick-and-Drake noise: “I don't like beef.”

Queenie Taua'aJune 1, 20242 min read

Lale, seeking solace by the beach to clear her head, stumbled upon a once-in-a-lifetime moment when she unexpectedly encountered the acclaimed rapper and producer J. Cole.

Their spontaneous meeting lasted about thirty minutes, with J. Cole engrossed in his work on a forthcoming album, as evidenced by the laptop beside him in the photo Lale shared on social media.

“All glory goes to God! I'm just a huge fan of J. Cole.”

During their brief but meaningful chat, Lale brought up the hot topic of the alleged feud between Kendrick Lamar and Drake. J. Cole, known for his modesty, expressed his dislike for such conflicts with a simple statement: “I don't like beef.”

For Lale, this unexpected meeting was a true blessing, a moment she'll forever cherish as a dedicated fan of J. Cole. What were the chances? It's a once-in-a-lifetime experience for Lale. Talk about being in the right place at the right time!

Amidst the chaos of the world, it's crucial to safeguard your peace and pursue what brings you joy — like going to the beach to clear your head.

“No such thing as a life that's better than yours.”
