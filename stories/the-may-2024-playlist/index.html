The May 2024 Playlist

Five throwbacks and five fresh tracks — nostalgia and new beats from Pasifika artists worldwide.

Queenie Taua'aMay 1, 20242 min read

Looking to freshen up your playlist or indulge in some nostalgic tunes? Look no further! We've carefully curated a playlist featuring 10 songs: 5 that evoke nostalgia for many of us and 5 tracks released this year — a blend of cherished memories and fresh beats from talented artists! We're excited to witness the continuous flow of new music from Pasifika artists worldwide. Dive into these 10 hits and be sure to add them to your rotation if you haven't already!

  1. Gotta Know Your Name — Rebel Souljahz
  2. Butterflies — Trey Smoov x Fiji
  3. Lifetime Lover — J Boog
  4. Undercover Lover — Three Plus
  5. Take Me Away — Laga Savea
  6. When He Was Mine (Pele Fo'i Mai) — A.R.T
  7. Late Night — Victor J Sefo x Etu
  8. Without You — Young Go
  9. Wasted Time Pt. 2 — Thee Xclusive
  10. Life Saver — Enoch x Tyra Vanderbeek
