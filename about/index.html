About PMN+ · Since 2017

Our stories.
Our people.
One ocean.

Polynesian Music Network+ is a Pasifika media platform that has grown well beyond its name. Music, sports, culture, news and the moments our community shares, told by us, for us, wherever we are in the world.

Where we started

From music to a movement

Polynesian Music began in June 2017 as a home for the sounds of the Pacific. Fans came for the music and stayed for each other. Over the years the conversation grew to the games we watch together, the culture we carry, the news from home and the people making us proud.

So we grew with it. PMN+ keeps the name our community knows and adds the plus for everything beyond the music. Today our team is on the ground at the biggest games, concerts and community events, bringing you inside access to the Pacific.

Why we do it

Like the ocean, social media connects us. It closes the distance between islands, cities and generations.

Our people live everywhere, from Apia and Nukuʻalofa to Auckland, Sydney, Salt Lake City and Los Angeles. Mainstream media too often overlooks our stories. PMN+ puts them front and centre, so wherever you are, home is never far.

To unite and strengthen the Pasifika community through media: uplifting the stories mainstream channels overlook, and giving our people coverage and representation worldwide.
