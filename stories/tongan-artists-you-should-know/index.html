Tongan Artists You Should Know

From Baby Joe to Diamond Langi — the Tongan voices shaping Pasifika music right now.

Queenie Taua'aNovember 22, 20264 min read

Tonga's music scene is having a moment. From viral TikTok tracks to festival stages, a new generation of Tongan and Tongan-heritage artists is carrying the language, the island and the sound to the world. Here are the names to know.

Baby Joe

The 25-year-old from East Palo Alto weaves Tongan into every verse — for him, Tongan Language Week is every week. Read his story.

Trixx

At 20, Patrick Jesse Taukolo turned the Halataufa'ahau road into his debut single — feel-good music straight from the island. Meet Trixx.

Advertisement

K'Nova

His first Tongan track — a nostalgic, mixtape-era single with Baby Joe — took over Pasifika TikTok. Hear the story.

Diamond Langi

Miss Universe New Zealand turned recording artist, the Tongan multi-hyphenate released her debut single "Keep You Here" as a letter to her late father. Read more.

Dinah Jane

The Tongan-American star turned a love song into an ancestor call on "Ocean Song," backed by an all-Polynesian cast. Go behind the scenes.

Kiridox

The Tongan-Kiribati melodic rapper from Wellington learned his languages through music on his journey from Kalo Block to the world. Read his journey.
